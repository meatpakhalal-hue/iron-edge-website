About Iron Edge

From the butcher's cutting floor to kitchens across the GTA.

My story

I grew up working behind the counter and on the cutting floor of my family's butcher shop, Pak Halal Meat, here in Mississauga.

A sharp knife isn't a luxury; it's what makes the work safe, fast and clean.

Years of boning beef, lamb and chicken taught me that lesson early. I started sharpening our own shop's knives, spending countless hours learning on whetstones, belt grinders and scissor equipment until I got every edge right.

Before long, other butchers and restaurants were asking me to sharpen theirs, and that's how Iron Edge was born.

Today I sharpen for kitchens, shops and home cooks across the GTA, and I treat every blade like it's going back on my own cutting board.

Our promise

If you're not happy with the edge, we'll resharpen it for free. No fuss.
