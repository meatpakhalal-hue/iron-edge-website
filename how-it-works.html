How It Works

Two easy ways to get your knives and scissors sharpened in Mississauga and the GTA.

Drop-off (next-day pickup)

  1. Bring your knives to Pak Halal Meat

    1201 Britannia Rd West, Mississauga, ON L5V 1N2
    Drop-offs Tuesday – Sunday, 10 AM – 8 PM (closed Mondays)

  2. Wrap and label them

    Make them safe to carry: wrap them in cloth, tape cardboard over the blade, or use a secure box. Label them with your name and phone number, or we'll label them for you when you drop them off.

  3. We sharpen them overnight

    We collect, sharpen, and return them to Pak Halal Meat the next day. Sunday drop-offs are ready on Monday.

  4. Pick up and pay

    Collect your blades and pay by cash or e-transfer.

Frequently asked questions

What if I can't pick up the next day?

No problem. We'll hold your items at Pak Halal Meat for 2 weeks. If they haven't been collected by then, we'll get in touch with you.

Do you sharpen hair-cutting shears?

Not at the moment, but we may offer it in the future. We also don't sharpen hair trimmer or clipper blades, or chainsaws.

How much does it cost?

Prices start from $8 per knife. See our Services & Pricing page for the full list.

Do I need to book before dropping off?

No. Just bring your items to Pak Halal Meat Tuesday to Sunday, between 10 AM and 8 PM. You only need to book for on-site visits.

I dropped off on a Sunday. When can I pick up?

Monday, as normal. Sunday drop-offs are still ready the next day. We don't take drop-offs on Mondays, but you can pick up as usual.

How should I wrap my knives?

Just make them safe enough to bring to us. You could wrap them in cloth, tape cardboard over the blade, or put them in a secure box. Label them with your name and phone number, or we'll do it for you when you bring them in.

How do I pay?

Pay by cash or e-transfer when you pick up your items.

Can you fix chipped or broken knives?

Yes. Repairs start from $5, depending on the damage.

Which areas do you visit for on-site sharpening?

We cover the GTA, on Mondays. The visit fee starts from $10, depending on how far we travel. Request a visit.
